DeckDiag: know your DJ gear works before it matters.

An app that walks you through every button, fader, knob and output of your players, mixers and turntables, then gives you a clear result and a report you can share.

In beta testing on iPhone, iPad and Mac. No account, no ads, no tracking. Made by TheFixer.

Before the showCheck a unit in a few minutes, one tap per test, and finish with a clear ready or not-ready result. Check a whole booth under one event.
Service and repairFull diagnostics with a before-and-after comparison and a PDF service report for the customer, with photos of faults.
Buying or selling used gearCheck every control before money changes hands, and send the buyer the saved report.

Join the waiting list

Leave your email and we write to you when DeckDiag is ready to download. That is all we use it for.

What it checks

  • Buttons, pads, faders, knobs, jog wheels and outputs, one guided test at a time
  • Firmware version compared with a built-in reference
  • Liquid-damage inspection with photo evidence
  • A parts reference for supported models, for repair technicians
  • Results saved on your device; you decide who gets the report

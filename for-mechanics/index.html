Mechanic Job Log Book

The paper record that stays next to the car: what came in, what was done, what was fitted.

  • One page per job. Customer, vehicle, the problem reported and the work done, in the same place every time.
  • Parts and labor on the record. What was fitted and how long it took, so the invoice is quick to write afterwards.
  • Customer sign-off. A signature line on every job record, useful when a repair is questioned later.
  • Built for the workshop. 120 pages with 108 job records, in a large format that is easy to write in.

Travel and setup time is charged once per visit, so a half day carries the same travel as a full day. Works in any currency. Add sales tax or VAT on top if you are registered for it. Nothing you type leaves this page.

Why travel and setup count once per visit

Loading the van, driving out and setting up take the same time whether you stay two hours or eight. Charging that time once per visit keeps a half day worth doing and means a full day is not overpriced.

Should a full day be cheaper?

A booked full day saves you quoting, extra driving and gaps between jobs, so some trades give a small discount for it. Leave the discount at zero if your diary is already full; you do not need to cut your rate to win a day you would fill anyway.

Set a half-day minimum

A half-day minimum stops short jobs from blocking out a morning for very little money. Put it on your estimate and booking terms so clients know it before you arrive.

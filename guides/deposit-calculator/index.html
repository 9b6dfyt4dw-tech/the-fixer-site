Deposit Calculator: Split a Job Into Deposit and Stage Payments

TheFixer Designs guide · 2026-11-09

A deposit protects you before you buy materials or block out days in your calendar, and stage payments keep a long job paid as it goes. This free calculator splits any job total into a deposit, equal stage payments and a final payment on completion, so your quote can state each amount clearly.

How much deposit is normal

Many service businesses ask for 20 to 50 percent up front, often enough to cover materials and the first days of work. Small jobs may need no deposit at all; custom or large jobs usually do. Whatever you choose, write it into the quote before the client says yes.

Stage payments on longer jobs

For work that runs over several weeks, tie each payment to a clear milestone, such as "rough-in complete" or "first draft delivered", rather than a date alone. The client sees progress before paying, and you are never far behind on cash.

Holding back a final amount

Some clients expect a small part, often 5 to 10 percent, to be paid only after a final walk-through or sign-off. Agreeing that up front avoids an argument at the end. Set it to 0 if your terms do not include it.

Only charge what you agreed

Late interest and fees are easiest to collect when they were written into your quote or invoice terms before the work started, for example "1.5% per month on overdue balances". Many places also cap how much interest a business may charge, so check the rules where you work before adding a fee.

How the number is worked out

The monthly rate is split into a daily rate over 30 days, then multiplied by the unpaid amount and the full days past the due date. That is simple interest: it does not compound. A flat fee, if your terms have one, is added once on top.

Put it in the reminder

State the original amount, the days overdue, the interest and any fee, and the new total, then give one easy way to pay. A calm, specific message gets paid faster than a vague one.

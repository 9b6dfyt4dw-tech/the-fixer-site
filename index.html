FAQ

Good questions.

Still unsure? Email us before you buy.

What software do I need?

Invoice templates and Business Kits include an Excel workbook (.xlsx) and fillable PDFs that work in free PDF readers such as Adobe Acrobat Reader. Social media templates and stream screens are SVG files: open them in a vector editor like Inkscape (free), Figma or Adobe Illustrator. Every pack also includes PNG previews.

Can I edit them in Canva?

Our templates are editable SVG and spreadsheet files, not Canva-linked templates. Some editors import SVG differently, so we recommend Inkscape, Figma or Illustrator for full text editing.

How do I get my files?

Right after checkout, the store you bought from gives you the download. On Etsy it is under Purchases; Payhip and Gumroad email you a download link.

Can I use them for my business?

Yes. Each purchase includes a license to edit and use the files in your own business, games, videos and creative work, including commercial projects. You can't resell or share the original files themselves.

Will the invoice add up the totals for me?

Yes. Enter your prices once in the Services tab, pick items from a dropdown and the workbook fills in rates and calculates tax, totals and balance due. The fillable PDFs calculate as you type too.

Something isn't working. What now?

Email eric@the-fixer.org with your order details and we'll help you sort it out.

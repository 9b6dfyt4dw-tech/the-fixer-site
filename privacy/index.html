Privacy Policy

TheFixer Designs · the-fixer.org · last updated November 1, 2026

This site is a catalogue. It shows our templates and links to the stores where you buy them. We keep it simple: no accounts, no cookies, no advertising trackers, and no personal data collected by this site unless you choose to join our email list (below).

What this site records

When a page loads, our own visit counter records the page path, the website that referred you (host name only), whether the device is a phone or a desktop, and a short daily visitor token. The token is a one-way hash of the day, a secret salt, your IP address and browser type; it cannot be reversed into your IP address and it changes every day. We use these counts only to see which pages and products get attention. Nothing is sold or shared.

When you click a store link, the same counter records which store and product the click went to, so we know which products people look at. Again, nothing personal is stored.

What this site does not do

This site sets no cookies, uses no third-party analytics, and runs no advertising pixels. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the standard request data any web font request involves; see Google's privacy policy for details.

Purchases

All purchases, payments and downloads happen on Etsy, Payhip, Gumroad or Amazon, under their own terms and privacy policies. Those stores handle your payment details and your order history. We only see what the store shows a seller, such as an order number and the item bought, and we use it solely to deliver and support your purchase. Refunds and cancellations follow the policy of the store you bought from, not ours.

Email

If you email us, we keep the conversation so we can help you. We do not add you to any mailing list unless you sign up yourself.

Email list (optional). On our free template page you can tick a box and leave your email address to get new free templates and occasional offers, about twice a month at most. Downloading the free template never requires it. We store only your email address, the date you signed up and which consent text you agreed to; not your IP address or browser. The address is sent in the body of a secure request, never in a web address, and is kept privately on our own computer, not with a mailing-list company. We never sell or share it. To unsubscribe, reply STOP to any email or write to us, and we delete your address.

Contact

TheFixer Designs · eric@the-fixer.org. Questions about this policy or a request to delete an email conversation are answered at that address.

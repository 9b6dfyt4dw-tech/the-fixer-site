What I do

Four things, done properly.

Board-level repair & micro-soldering

Component-level diagnosis and repair of electronics that would otherwise be written off: micro-soldering, rework, electrical fault-finding and careful modification, with the original hardware kept intact wherever possible.

  • Phones, laptops, controllers, instruments and industrial boards
  • Fault isolation with the schematic or without one
  • Reversible modifications and upgrades

Reverse engineering & custom firmware

Understanding undocumented devices and giving them new jobs: reading out and mapping hardware, writing firmware for microcontrollers (ESP32 and similar), and adding displays, sensors or connectivity to existing products.

  • Protocol and interface analysis
  • Firmware for ESP32-class devices, Wi-Fi, BLE, sensors
  • Working prototypes, not slide decks

On-premises AI automation

Multi-agent systems that run on your own hardware, so your data stays in the building. TheFixer Designs itself is run this way: a team of local AI agents researches, drafts, quality-checks and publishes the catalog.

  • Research, drafting, QA and publishing pipelines
  • Open-source models on your own GPUs; no cloud API keys
  • Audit logs for every decision an agent makes

Rapid hardware prototyping

From idea to a working piece in days: microcontroller boards, round and rectangular displays, LED lighting, 3D-printed carriers and housings, battery and USB-C power.

  • Proof-of-concept builds and wearables
  • Fit checks for existing enclosures
  • Documentation that another engineer can pick up

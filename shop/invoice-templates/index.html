FAQ

Good questions.

Still unsure? Email us before you buy.

How are these different from a generic invoice template?

The services dropdown already lists the jobs that trade sells, with unit and rate, so an invoice takes a few clicks instead of typing every line.

Does it calculate tax and totals?

Yes. Subtotal, tax, discount, amount paid and balance due calculate automatically in the workbook, and the fillable PDFs do the same when you type.

Can I add my logo and match my colours?

Yes. Business name, address, payment terms and your price list are set once on the Setup sheet and flow into every invoice and estimate. The accent colour is a normal cell fill, so it changes to your brand colour in a few clicks, and your logo goes in beside your business name with Insert > Image.

FAQ

Good questions.

Still unsure? Email us before you buy.

What do I need to edit the SVG files?

A vector editor: Inkscape (free), Adobe Illustrator or Affinity Designer. Install the included fonts first, then type over the sample text and swap the photo.

Are these Canva templates?

No. They are editable SVG files with PNG previews. They do not import into Canva as editable designs.

Can I change the colours to match my brand?

Yes, in one step: every pack includes Brand It, a small page that opens in your browser (offline, nothing is uploaded). Drop in your logo and your brand colours are applied to every design, with your logo placed on each one. Every shape and text is also a normal vector object, so you can recolour anything by hand.

FAQ

Good questions.

Still unsure? Email us before you buy.

What do I need to edit the SVG files?

A vector editor: Inkscape (free), Adobe Illustrator or Affinity Designer. Install the included fonts first, then type over the sample text and swap the photo.

Are these Canva templates?

No. They are editable SVG files with PNG previews. They do not import into Canva as editable designs.

Can I change the colours?

Yes. Every shape and text is a normal vector object, so colours, fonts and layout are fully editable.

1Five things to settle before January

  • Start a fresh invoice number series. Pick a simple pattern such as 2027-001 and keep it for the whole year, so every invoice is easy to find again.
  • Put your payment terms on every invoice. When payment is due and how to pay. Written once in the template, it is on every invoice you send.
  • Write your services and rates down once. One list you copy from, instead of working out each quote from memory.
  • Keep last year in one folder. Every invoice and estimate from this year in one place before the new ones start.
  • Choose one invoicing day a week. A fixed day to send invoices and check which ones are still open.

General housekeeping ideas, not tax or legal advice.
